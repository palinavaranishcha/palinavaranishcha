About
the artist

I’m Palina Varanishcha, an artist working through color, texture, and emotion.

My paintings are a way of exploring feelings, memories, and the quiet moments that often stay beneath the surface.

I’m interested in the connection between color and emotion, and in creating works that leave space for each viewer to find their own meaning.

For me, painting is an intuitive process — a conversation between the idea, the material, and the moment.

Welcome to my world.
